Measure the screen diagonally, corner to corner of the lit area, or use the number the laptop was sold with.

How far away you sit

Sit the way you normally would, look at the screen, and hold still. Then press Space — one second of samples sets the scale of everything else.

Check it

Move your head left and right. The reading should follow you in centimetres, and the room behind the glass should shift as if you were leaning past a window frame. Sitting still, you should be looking into the middle of the room: it is tilted to face wherever you were when you pressed Space.
